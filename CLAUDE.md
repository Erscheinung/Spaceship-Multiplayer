# NEON WING project guidance

Read `agents.md` for project architecture, standing art direction and the resume log.

Approach visual work as a game designer, art director and developer. Build a coherent, charming world with deliberate silhouettes, materials, signs, motifs and lived-in details. Procedural systems must arrange a designed kit; random variation, generic tiled blocks and unfinished AI-generated assets are not an acceptable finish. Texture and shaders should strengthen form, story and gameplay readability.

Use the illustrated Afterlight Skyway identity and the visual references described in `docs/art-direction.md`. Assess the result in motion, through boost and turns, on portrait and desktop, and in day/night lighting. Preserve batching, rolling geometry, collision boundaries and the frame budget. Validate visual quality and performance before claiming completion.
