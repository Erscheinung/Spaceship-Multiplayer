# Afterlight material atlas

The selected source asset is [afterlight-materials.png](../static/textures/afterlight-materials.png). It was created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project, then inspected and copied into the repository. The app loads the [WebP delivery asset](../static/textures/afterlight-materials.webp), encoded at quality 92: approximately 544 KB rather than the source PNG's 3.1 MB. The source and delivery image are 1254 × 1254 pixels; runtime preparation packs the sixteen cells into a shared 1024 × 1024 GPU texture with isolated padding and mip levels. It is a surface atlas, not a replacement background image or a pasted building photograph.

The art direction follows the material contrasts and colour relationships in the user's Sunset Town concept references: warm limewash, painted timber, brick and stone, cool glazed ceramics, clay and slate roofs, patterned textiles and worn paving. Material choice belongs to each property's construction identity. Colour variation should preserve the authored surface rather than multiply it by arbitrary saturated tints.

The runtime shares one material atlas across the city. Do not create image copies per property, update it every frame, or replace filtered material detail with distant high-frequency noise. Loading must have a safe fallback, and asynchronous completion must not revive disposed scene resources.
